Medical Biostatistics Teaching Lab

Visual, case-based, and R-supported teaching materials for medical biostatistics.

A teaching lab for medical and health-science students who need to understand statistical ideas through clinical examples, visual explanations, and short R activities.

Created by: Mahdi Akbarzadeh Student-friendly modules Self-contained R activities

Course design

This teaching lab is designed to help students move from calculation to interpretation. Each module connects a statistical idea with a medical example, a visual explanation, a common mistake, and a short R activity.

How students use this lab

  1. Open a module and read the teaching page.
  2. Review the visual explanation and common mistake.
  3. Open the R activity and run it in RStudio.
  4. Check the Console output to find where the figure was saved.
  5. Answer the student interpretation task in your own words.
