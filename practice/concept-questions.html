Sample question

In a clinical dataset, the mean is much larger than the median. What is the most likely interpretation?

  1. The data are probably right-skewed.
  2. The data are definitely normal.
  3. The data definitely have no outliers.
  4. The standard deviation must be zero.
Correct answer: option 1. A mean much larger than the median is often compatible with a right-skewed distribution.
