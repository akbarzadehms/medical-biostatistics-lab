Descriptive statistics

  1. A dataset is strongly right-skewed. Which summary is usually more appropriate: mean ± SD or median and IQR? Explain why.
  2. Two laboratory markers have different units. Why can the coefficient of variation be more useful than the standard deviation for comparison?
  3. Why does a value above Q3 not automatically mean that the value is an outlier?

Normal distribution

  1. In an approximately normal distribution, why are the mean and median usually close to each other?
  2. What does it mean if about 95% of values fall within mean ± 2 SD?
  3. Why should normality be treated as a model rather than a guaranteed property of medical data?

Z-scores and percentiles

  1. A patient has a Z-score of +1.5 for a measurement. Interpret this result in one sentence.
  2. A student is at the 80th percentile. What does this mean, and what does it not mean?
  3. Why is a raw score not enough for comparison unless the reference distribution is known?
