Formula, use case, and common mistake in one place.

Concept Formula Use case Common mistake
IQR IQR = Q3 - Q1 Robust spread of the middle 50% of data Assuming every value above Q3 is an outlier
CV CV = SD / Mean × 100 Comparing relative variability Comparing only raw SD across different scales
Variance Mean of squared deviations from the mean Mathematical measure of dispersion Interpreting variance in the original unit
Standard deviation SD = sqrt(variance) Spread around the mean in the original unit Calling SD the mean absolute distance from the mean
